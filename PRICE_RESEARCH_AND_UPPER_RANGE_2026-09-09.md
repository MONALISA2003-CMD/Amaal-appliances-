# Amaal price policy — 2026-09-09

All storefront prices use normal retail/list pricing, not discounted promotional prices. Where an exact current Uganda price was available, the normal/list or upper-normal local price was preferred. Where only international or marketplace evidence was available, pricing was set conservatively within the upper-normal Uganda retail range for the exact model/specification.
