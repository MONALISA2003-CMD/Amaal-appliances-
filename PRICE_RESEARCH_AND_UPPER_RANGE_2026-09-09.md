# Amaal Appliances — Price Repricing & Brand Registry Cleanup

Date: 2026-09-09

## Objective
Reprice the full 291-product catalogue using current Uganda retail-market evidence, targeting the **upper normal retail range rather than temporary/discounted selling prices**.

## Method
- Existing product prices were treated as the catalogue's researched baseline.
- Current Uganda retail evidence was checked against Jumia Uganda and Uganda appliance retailers.
- Where an exact model had a visible non-sale/list/maximum retail benchmark, that benchmark was used or closely rounded.
- For the remaining products, category-specific upper-range uplift factors were applied to the existing researched baseline, with stronger uplifts for small appliances where discounting is especially common.
- Prices were rounded into customer-friendly UGX bands rather than leaving arbitrary low-sale values.
- No product records were added or removed.

## Important pricing policy
These are intended as **upper-range catalogue selling prices**, not claims that every item is currently sold at exactly that amount by every retailer. Discount campaigns, seller warranty, stock, import costs and model-year differences can change live market prices.
