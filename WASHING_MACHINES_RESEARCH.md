# Washing Machines Research

Initial catalogue build covers the agreed washing-machine brands: Samsung, LG, TCL, Roch, Midea and SPJ.

## Current implementation
- 8 washing-machine SKUs added.
- Exact model identifiers are retained where the Uganda listing provides them.
- One master UGX price per SKU.
- Warranty is only shown when supported by the local listing.
- Images are tied to the exact product listing; imageClean is false where the available primary image needs a later visual replacement.

## Coverage remains open
Expand each agreed brand with additional verified models, especially Samsung, TCL and SPJ, then perform a visual image audit and final price/specification audit before declaring the family complete.
