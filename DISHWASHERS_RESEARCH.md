# Dishwashers Research — Amaal Appliances

Research date: 2026-09-08

## Selection method

OEM/manufacturer information was checked first, then Uganda/local retailer evidence was used for pricing and market availability. Products were selected to cover budget through premium and to avoid duplicate configurations.

## Image standard

All seven implemented SKUs use clean manufacturer-hosted images or manufacturer-controlled product assets. No seller watermark, phone number or promotional overlay is intentionally used. Full product images are displayed using the existing `object-fit: contain` product-image presentation.
