## Pricing method
1. Identify the exact model/SKU, not merely a product category or similar-looking appliance.
2. Prefer multiple credible Uganda observations when available.
3. Use OEM/regional product information to verify the model and specifications.
4. Normalize awkward retail values to a sensible UGX storefront value where the research record supports it.
5. Treat large discrepancies as a research task, not an automatic average.
6. Freeze the chosen implementation price only after model identity and source quality are acceptable.
7. Re-check price before production launch or when a stale observation is suspected.

## Confidence meaning
- **High:** exact model is well identified and evidence is strong, often with multiple Uganda observations and/or OEM confirmation.
- **Medium:** exact model is identified with useful regional/OEM evidence and a credible local observation.
- **Limited:** usable fallback evidence exists but the research should be strengthened before a production price is frozen.

## Important implementation rule
The `price` field in the current frontend is catalogue data, not an authority for a production order. The future backend must calculate/validate the payable amount from its own current SKU master before creating an order.
