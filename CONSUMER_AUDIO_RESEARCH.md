## Implemented

### Hisense HS2100
- 2.1-channel soundbar.
- 240W total output; wireless subwoofer; DTS Virtual:X; Dolby Audio; Bluetooth; HDMI ARC; USB; optical; AUX.
- Uganda evidence: Bayet UGX 520,000; Jumia Uganda also lists the exact model.
- OEM: Hisense official product/technical page.
- Image: clean exact-model image from Wootware; no promotional overlay was selected.

- Uganda evidence: Jumia Uganda observations around UGX 880,000–1,080,000.
- OEM: Hisense official technical page.

### LG S65TR
- LG 5.1-channel soundbar system.

## Researched but held
Samsung HW-C450 and JBL Bar 2.1 Deep Bass were verified as real models with Uganda market evidence, but were held from this implementation because the available image URL evidence did not meet the direct-image/host consolidation gate for this ZIP.

## Duplicate control
The existing catalogue already contained Geepas GMS8516, Xdobo X7 and Oraimo SpaceBox Active. New soundbar records were therefore selected as distinct models/configurations.
