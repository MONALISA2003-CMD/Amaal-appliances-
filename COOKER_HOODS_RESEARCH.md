# Cooker Hoods Research

## Research method

OEM/manufacturer pages were used for product specifications where available. Uganda/local retailers were used for current local price and availability observations. Retailers used in this pass include TilyExpress, Dombelo and Jumia Uganda, with Newmatic Uganda treated as the official Uganda distributor/service centre for Newmatic products.

## Image standard

Implemented images must be clean and non-watermarked. No seller phone numbers, promotional banners, screenshots, or wrong-model substitutions are accepted. OEM imagery is preferred; a clean exact-product local retailer image is acceptable when an OEM image is not available.
