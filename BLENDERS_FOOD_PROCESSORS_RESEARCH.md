# Blenders & Food Processors Research

Implemented as an expansion of the existing Blenders / Blenders & Juicers catalogue.

## Research sources
- TilyExpress Uganda: Saachi blender catalogue and exact model pages.
- TilyExpress Uganda: Geepas exact model listings including GSB6104, GSB9891 and GSB44017.
- TilyExpress Uganda: Midea BL1197A and MJ-BL6006W exact model pages.
- TilyExpress Uganda: Globalstar GS-1589 exact model listing.
- TilyExpress Uganda: Hoffmans 10-in-1 HM-108B listing.
- Jumia Uganda: additional Hoffmans model and local price cross-checks.

## Duplication control
Existing product records were checked by ID, slug, model and brand/model/category fingerprint before insertion. No duplicate IDs, slugs or models were introduced.

## Image standard
Only exact-model product imagery from the researched retailer listings was used. No promotional banner was selected as the product image and no new image URL was intentionally taken from a page URL. The project image audit reports all product records with direct HTTPS image URLs and no known watermark URL markers.

## Pricing
Prices are Amaal catalogue prices based on the researched Uganda market observations, not claims of fixed manufacturer MSRP.
