# Ovens Research — Amaal Appliances

## Implementation batch

Three exact oven SKUs were implemented after OEM and Uganda-market verification. Existing products were not removed.

### 1. Newmatic FM6113T
- OEM: Newmatic official product page / Uganda official distributor.
- Exact model: FM6113T.
- 65L built-in electric oven, 11 functions, digital touch control, cooling fan, tangential ventilation, triple-layer cool door, Class A.
- Uganda price observation: UGX 2,800,000 from Newmatic Kitchen Appliances Uganda / Jumia and TilyExpress.
- Image: clean exact-model Newmatic product image, no watermark or promotional overlay.

### 2. Newmatic FE633
- OEM/Uganda distributor: Newmatic.
- Exact model: FE633.
- 65L built-in electric oven, cooling fan, stainless steel cavity, two-knob controls, two-layer glass door, tray and rack.
- Uganda price observation: UGX 1,600,000 from Newmatic Kitchen Appliances Uganda / Jumia and TilyExpress.
- Image: clean exact-model FE633 image from the product instruction-manual asset, no seller branding or promotional banner.

### 3. Midea MBO-M1865
- OEM: Midea official product page and catalogue.
- Exact model: MBO-M1865.
- 65L built-in electric oven, mechanical knobs, 8 functions with rotisserie, two-layer removable door, two universal trays and rack, 3000W, 595 × 575 × 595 mm.
- Uganda price observation: UGX 850,000 from Kanta Uganda's Official Midea Store listing.
- Image: direct Midea official product image, clean and non-watermarked.

## Excluded from this batch
- Newmatic FM6133T-PRO was researched and locally confirmed at UGX 3,500,000, but its currently exposed official product image contains a prominent Newmatic promotional strip. It was therefore NOT implemented until a clean image is available.
- Samsung NV60K5140BB/SG and LG WSEZM7225S2 were verified at OEM level but were not implemented because a current exact-model Uganda price observation was not sufficiently established in this research pass.

## Research rule
OEM sources establish model facts/specifications. Uganda retailers/distributors establish local price/availability. No model numbers are invented. No existing SKU is removed.
