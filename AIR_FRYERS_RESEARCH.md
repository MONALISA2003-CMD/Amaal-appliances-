# AIR FRYERS RESEARCH — Amaal Appliances

## Scope
Expanded the existing Quick Cooking category with non-repetitive air fryers, using OEM information first and Uganda/local retailer evidence for pricing and availability.

| Black + Decker | AF4037-B5 | Kanta Uganda, Fairprice Mall; Black+Decker official | UGX 560,000 |

## Image policy

Only clean product imagery was selected. No seller watermarks, phone-number overlays or promotional banners were intentionally used. A clean local retailer image was accepted when OEM imagery was not practical. Thumbnail URLs were upgraded to larger renditions where the source supported it.

## Research notes

Midea's official product page confirms MF-CY85WK2, 8.5L capacity and 1900–2200W rated power. Uganda pricing was cross-checked against Fairprice Mall.

Hisense H09AFBK2S5 was cross-checked across Life Stores, TilyExpress and Dombelo. The Life Stores image was selected because it is a clean exact-model product image.

Geepas GAF37525UK was cross-checked against Geepas's official catalogue and Uganda retailers. The Weafmall image is a clean exact-model product photograph.

Kenwood HFP70.000BK was cross-checked with TilyExpress Uganda, including the exact 8L / 4L+4L configuration and 1700W specification.

Black + Decker AF4037-B5 was verified against the official Black+Decker product page and Uganda retailer evidence. The official product image is used.

## Deliberately excluded

Beko FRL5388B and Tefal EY905D40 were researched but not added in this pass because the available image endpoints did not meet the required image-quality/reliability standard. No questionable image was substituted merely to increase SKU count.

## Additional expansion — September 2026

Three further non-repetitive air-fryer SKUs were added after a second Uganda market research pass:

| Brand | Model | Distinctive position | Uganda evidence | Amaal price |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| ADH | AF0604D | 6L digital budget model | Kanta Uganda; Jumia Uganda Official Store | UGX 220,000 |
| Philips | NA352/00 | 9L dual-basket premium model | Kanta Uganda; Philips official | UGX 740,000 |
| Saachi | NL-AF-4783 | 12L oven-style 11-function model | Life Stores Uganda | UGX 610,000 |

ADH AF0604D is listed by Kanta Uganda at UGX 220,000 with 6L capacity, 1200W power, 80–200°C temperature control and a 60-minute timer. Jumia Uganda's Official Store also carries the exact AF0604D model. The selected image is the clean exact-model Jumia product image; no promotional overlay was selected.

Philips NA352/00 is confirmed by Philips as the 3000 Series Dual-Basket Airfryer with 9L capacity, two baskets, eight presets and synchronized cooking. Kanta Uganda currently lists the exact 9L model at UGX 740,000. The selected image is an official Philips product asset and was visually checked as clean product imagery.

Saachi NL-AF-4783 is listed by Life Stores Uganda at UGX 610,000. The exact model is documented as a 12L digital air fryer oven with 11 presets, digital touchscreen, rapid hot-air circulation and 1800W power. The selected Life Stores image is the clean exact-model product photograph without seller watermark or promotional overlay.

### Deliberately rejected during this expansion

Globalstar GS-K1445 and GS-K7045 were researched but not added because the readily available exact-model Jumia imagery contains a prominent warranty/promotional graphic. This fails Amaal's image rule even though the products themselves are otherwise suitable.

Tefal EY901840 was also researched and locally verified, but its strongest local product image endpoint returned 404 during validation, so it was not added rather than introducing a potentially broken image URL.
