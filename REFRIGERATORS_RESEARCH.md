## Research decisions
- The customer-facing price is a single normalized UGX master price.
- Promotional crossed-out prices are not copied into the storefront.
- Specifications for LG and Samsung are anchored to manufacturer pages and checked against Uganda listings.
- Midea, Roch and ADH records use exact-model/local listing evidence.
- Beko and additional LG/TCL/Samsung/Midea/Roch/ADH models remain research candidates until a direct, model-matched clean image and acceptable Uganda price evidence are both secured.
- No refrigerator was added merely because a similar-looking image was available.
