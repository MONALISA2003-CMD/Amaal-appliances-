# Amaal Appliances — Global Deep Research Batch 14

Date: 2026-09-09

## Scope
Deep global re-research of the 12 remaining catalogue products with missing `image` fields in the Batch-13 archive. Research covered Uganda retailers/marketplaces plus international retailer/manufacturer sources. Under the updated project rule, an exact SKU image was preferred; where a clean exact image was not available, a visually matching generic/same-family image with no visible conflicting model number was accepted.
